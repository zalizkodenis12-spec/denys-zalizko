Work
Process

5 steps from idea to live website. Zero uncertainty — you know exactly what happens at every stage.

A brief discovery call (10-15 minutes) where we learn about your business, goals, and wishes in detail. We discuss the future site structure, key functionality, and required integrations. Afterwards, we develop a free demo version so you can immediately see our approach.

10-15 minutes

Within 1-2 days, we create a core draft containing the hero screen, overall layout, and primary design. You can test and interact with it live in your browser. This saves time and ensures we are on the same page before full development starts.

1-2 days

For mutual trust, an advance payment of 1/10th of the project cost is made (for example, $300 on a $3,000 project).

~15 minutes

After the advance payment, we connect again. You provide your comments, and we make all necessary refinements. We fully adapt the design, colors, and typography to your brand. This phase continues until you are 100% satisfied with the final result.

1 day

At this stage, the final payment is completed. Once everything is polished, we optimize the code for high performance and set up fundamental SEO. We assist with domain setup, hosting connection, and deployment. You receive a fully ready-to-run business tool that delivers customers.
