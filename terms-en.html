1. General Provisions

These Terms of Service govern the relationship between the client (hereinafter — "Client") and the web development studio DenisWeb Studio (hereinafter — "Contractor") regarding the design, development, optimization, and launch of websites.

2. Ordering Process & Development Stages

Our collaboration process consists of 5 clear steps:

  1. Brief: A short discovery call (10-15 minutes) to understand business goals, design expectations, and required functionality.
  2. Demo Version: Creating a free initial draft of the site (hero screen, layout, styling) within 1-2 days, which the Client can review live in a browser.
  3. Advance Payment: To establish mutual trust and commence full development, an advance payment of 1/10th (10%) of the total project cost is completed.
  4. Revisions & Refinements: We reconnect, gather Client feedback, and implement all requested adjustments. Design and colors are fully tailored to your brand.
  5. Launch: Completion of final payment, performance optimization, basic SEO setup, domain assistance, deployment, and handover of full access.

3. Payment & Settlement

Payments for services are made via cashless settlement or other mutually agreed payment methods in two stages:

  • Advance (10%) — paid upon approval of the demo version before full development starts.
  • Final Balance (90%) — paid upon completion of all development work, revisions, and prior to the final transfer of site assets and credentials.

Upon receipt of 100% payment, all exclusive property copyright to the custom design, source code, structure, and materials are fully transferred to the Client. The Contractor reserves the right to showcase the completed project in their portfolio.

5. Guarantees & Technical Support

The Contractor guarantees that the website meets modern web standards, loads quickly, displays impeccably on mobile devices and tablets, and provides basic post-launch technical support and consultations.
